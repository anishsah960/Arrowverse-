This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Puzzle ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you play our mobile and web application.

1. Information We Collect

We respect your privacy and strive to minimize data collection:

  • Game Progression & Scores: High scores, completed levels, stars earned, and visual theme preferences are stored locally on your device (and securely synced to cloud services like Firebase/Supabase if cloud backup is enabled).

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our game may display advertisements and utilize third-party SDKs to provide analytics, backend sync, and monetization. These third parties have their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: Used to display interstitial and banner advertisements. AdMob may collect anonymous advertising identifiers (GAID/IDFA) and device signals to deliver contextual or personalized ads in accordance with Google's advertising policies. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase: Used for cloud database synchronization and performance monitoring. Firebase Privacy Policy

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Arrow Puzzle is a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or request personal identifiable information (such as names, physical addresses, or phone numbers) from children under the age of 13.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game progress is stored directly on your device and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing the application cache/data in your device settings.
